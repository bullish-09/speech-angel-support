# SpeechAngel

**Your Private, On-Device Presentation Coach & Teleprompter**

## App Store Subtitle
Nail your presentation. No internet required.

## Description
SpeechAngel is the ultimate companion for public speakers, educators, and professionals. Whether you're pitching to investors, delivering a keynote, or running a team meeting, SpeechAngel acts as your invisible co-pilot, ensuring you hit every key point with confidence.

Powered by advanced, entirely on-device AI, SpeechAngel listens to your speech in real-time, tracks your progress through your slides, and provides gentle, proactive nudges if you miss important information—all without ever sending your sensitive data to the cloud.

### Key Features:

🎙️ **Smart Audio Tracking**
Upload your PDF or PPTX deck and let SpeechAngel listen. It automatically tracks your position in your speaker notes using lightning-fast semantic matching. 

🧠 **Proactive AI Coaching**
If you skip a crucial bullet point, SpeechAngel's local AI generates a soft, conversational nudge (e.g., "Don't forget the Q3 growth metrics") and whispers it to you via your earpiece.

⚙️ **Customizable Teleprompter & Coaching**
Tailor the experience to your speaking style with flexible, independent controls:
*   **Voice Prompting:** Toggle AI coaching on or off. Adjust the silence delay to determine exactly how long SpeechAngel waits before gently nudging you.
*   **Auto-Scroll (Listening-based):** Automatically scrolls your notes by listening and smartly tracking your current place in the speech.
*   **Auto-Scroll (Time-based):** Smoothly scrolls your notes at a configurable reading speed (Words Per Minute).
*   **Manual Control:** Disable auto-scrolling to take full manual control of your notes.
*   **Custom LLM Prompts:** Configure exactly how the on-device AI summarizes and prepares your raw slide notes into digestible coaching points.

📊 **Post-Presentation Grading & History**
Improve over time. After you finish speaking, SpeechAngel reviews your transcript and provides a detailed scorecard on:
*   **Content Accuracy:** Did you state the facts correctly?
*   **Content Completeness:** Did you cover all the main points?
*   **Delivery Pacing:** Were there long, awkward pauses?
*   **Delivery Fluency:** Did you hesitate or struggle through sentences?

🔒 **Privacy First, Always**
Your presentations are your business. SpeechAngel's powerful AI models run entirely offline on your device. No accounts, no cloud processing, no data harvesting.
